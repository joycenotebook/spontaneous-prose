A prepared speech for Toastmasters, and the first episode of Spontaneous Prose. The club topic was “a sense of ease.” Joyce reads what kept her up at night: urgent work versus what actually feels important, and why being present is so hard when the list never ends.

Takeaway: she does not have a solution. When the question gets too loud, she gets out of bed, turns on the light, and speaks it into a microphone.
